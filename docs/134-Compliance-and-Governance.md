# Kromic Store Backend Documentation

# Phase 06 -- 134 Compliance & Governance

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Approved Foundation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document establishes the compliance and governance framework for
Kromic Store. It defines policies, responsibilities, controls, and
oversight required to operate the platform securely, ethically, and in
accordance with applicable legal and contractual obligations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Objectives

-   Establish governance processes
-   Support regulatory compliance
-   Manage operational risk
-   Define accountability
-   Strengthen internal controls
-   Promote continuous compliance

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Governance Model

Governance should include:

-   Executive oversight
-   Engineering leadership
-   Security ownership
-   Operations management
-   Product governance

Each role must have clearly defined responsibilities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Regulatory Compliance

Consider applicable requirements such as:

-   GDPR
-   CCPA
-   SOC 2
-   ISO 27001
-   PCI DSS (when processing payments)
-   Local privacy regulations

Compliance obligations should be reviewed periodically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Risk Management

Maintain a risk register covering:

-   Security risks
-   Operational risks
-   Availability risks
-   Vendor risks
-   Data protection risks
-   Business continuity risks

Review risks regularly and assign owners.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Internal Controls

Implement controls for:

-   Access management
-   Change management
-   Backup verification
-   Security monitoring
-   Configuration management
-   Audit logging

Controls should be documented and periodically tested.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Policy Management

Maintain documented policies for:

-   Security
-   Acceptable use
-   Incident response
-   Data retention
-   Backup and recovery
-   Software development lifecycle

Review and approve policies annually.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Audit Readiness

Maintain evidence for:

-   Access reviews
-   Security scans
-   Vulnerability remediation
-   Deployment approvals
-   Backup testing
-   Incident investigations

Store evidence securely and make it easily retrievable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Third-Party Governance

Evaluate vendors for:

-   Security posture
-   Availability
-   Compliance certifications
-   Data handling
-   Contractual obligations

Review critical vendors on a recurring basis.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Compliance Monitoring

Track:

-   Policy exceptions
-   Audit findings
-   Control effectiveness
-   Security incidents
-   Compliance status
-   Risk remediation progress

Escalate unresolved issues promptly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Continuous Improvement

Perform:

-   Periodic internal audits
-   Policy reviews
-   Control assessments
-   Security awareness training
-   Lessons learned reviews

Use findings to strengthen governance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Document responsibilities clearly.
-   Review controls regularly.
-   Keep policies current.
-   Address audit findings promptly.
-   Treat compliance as an ongoing process.

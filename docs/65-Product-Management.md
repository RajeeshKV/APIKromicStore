# Kromic Store Frontend Documentation

# Phase 04 -- 65 Product Management

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Approved Foundation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

The Product Management module enables tenants to create, organize,
publish, and manage physical, digital, and service-based products from a
unified interface.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goals

-   Fast product creation
-   Powerful editing
-   Efficient inventory management
-   Flexible pricing
-   SEO-friendly products
-   Scalable catalog management

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module Overview

The module includes:

-   Product Listing
-   Product Editor
-   Product Variants
-   Inventory
-   Pricing
-   Media Gallery
-   Categories & Collections
-   SEO
-   Shipping
-   Publishing
-   Bulk Operations
-   Import & Export

------------------------------------------------------------------------

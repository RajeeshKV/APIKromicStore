# Kromic Store Frontend Documentation

# Phase 04 -- 70 Reports & Analytics

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Approved Foundation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

The Reports & Analytics module gives tenants comprehensive visibility
into business performance through interactive dashboards, operational
reports, financial summaries, and exportable insights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goals

-   Enable data-driven decisions
-   Monitor store performance
-   Identify growth opportunities
-   Support operational reporting
-   Provide flexible exports

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Module Overview

The module consists of:

-   Executive Dashboard
-   Sales Reports
-   Order Reports
-   Product Reports
-   Customer Reports
-   Inventory Reports
-   Financial Reports
-   Marketing Reports
-   Custom Reports
-   Scheduled Reports

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Dashboard

Display KPIs including:

-   Revenue
-   Orders
-   Customers
-   Conversion Rate
-   Average Order Value
-   Returning Customers
-   Inventory Value
-   Gross Profit (future)

Support comparisons against previous periods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sales Reports

Provide:

-   Daily Sales
-   Weekly Sales
-   Monthly Sales

-   Revenue by Product

Include trend charts and drill-down capabilities.

# Filters

Support:

-   Date Range
-   Product
-   Category
-   Customer Segment
-   Sales Channel

Persist active filters during the session.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Export & Scheduling

Export formats:

-   CSV
-   Excel
-   PDF

Scheduling:

-   Daily
-   Weekly

Support email delivery for scheduled reports.

# Visualizations

Support:

-   Line Charts
-   Bar Charts
-   Area Charts
-   Pie Charts
-   KPI Cards
-   Tables
-   Heat Maps (future)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Loading & Empty States

Provide:

-   Skeleton charts
-   Empty report messaging
-   Background export progress
-   Retry actions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Accessibility

Ensure:

-   Keyboard-accessible controls
-   Screen-reader summaries
-   High-contrast charts
-   Accessible tables

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Keep KPIs consistent across reports.
-   Make filters easy to discover.
-   Optimize large datasets with server-side processing.
-   Allow drill-down without losing context.
-   Make exports match on-screen data.

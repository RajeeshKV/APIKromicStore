# Kromic Store Backend Documentation

# Phase 06 -- 136 Business Continuity Plan

**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Approved Foundation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This document defines the Business Continuity Plan (BCP) for Kromic
Store. It provides guidance for maintaining essential services during
disruptive events and restoring normal operations with minimal impact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Objectives

-   Maintain critical business services
-   Minimize downtime
-   Protect customer data
-   Ensure operational resilience
-   Define recovery responsibilities
-   Continuously improve preparedness

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Business Continuity Principles

-   Prioritize safety and security
-   Protect critical services
-   Restore operations in phases
-   Communicate clearly
-   Validate recovery before resuming normal operations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Critical Business Functions

The following functions are considered critical:

-   Authentication
-   Storefront availability
-   Checkout and order processing
-   Payment integration
-   Tenant administration
-   Background processing
-   Monitoring and alerting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recovery Objectives

Define and periodically review:

-   Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
-   Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
-   Maximum Acceptable Downtime (MAD)

Objectives should align with business requirements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Disruption Scenarios

Plan for:

-   Infrastructure failure
-   Database outage
-   Cloud provider disruption
-   Network failure
-   Cybersecurity incident
-   Third-party service outage
-   Human error

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recovery Procedures

Recovery should follow these phases:

1.  Detect the incident
2.  Assess business impact
3.  Activate continuity procedures
4.  Restore critical services
5.  Validate functionality
6.  Resume normal operations
7.  Conduct post-incident review

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Communication Plan

Communicate with:

-   Engineering
-   Operations
-   Customer support
-   Leadership
-   Customers
-   External vendors

Provide regular status updates during major incidents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Alternate Operations

Where possible:

-   Use redundant infrastructure
-   Fail over to secondary services
-   Operate with reduced functionality
-   Queue non-critical workloads

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Roles & Responsibilities

Assign owners for:

-   Incident Commander
-   Engineering Lead
-   Operations Lead
-   Security Lead
-   Communications Coordinator

Maintain an up-to-date contact list.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Testing & Exercises

Conduct:

-   Tabletop exercises
-   Disaster recovery drills
-   Backup restoration tests
-   Failover validation
-   Communication exercises

Review results and improve procedures.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Best Practices

-   Keep continuity plans current.
-   Test regularly.
-   Document lessons learned.
-   Automate recovery where practical.
-   Review after every significant incident.
